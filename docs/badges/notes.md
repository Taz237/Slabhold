# Slabhold achievement badges: build notes

Everything is hand-built SVG, generated by `badges.py`. No AI images were used.
- To rebuild: `python3 badges.py step1` (ladder, grid, previews), then `python3 badges.py step2` (everything else).
- Rendering uses headless Chrome at 2x, then a LANCZOS downsample.
- Light comes from the top-left on every tier.

## How a badge is composed
- The canvas is 512 × 512 and transparent, with its center at (256, 256).
- Each `frame-<tier>.svg/png` holds a dark circular **icon well** at the center, radius **104**.
- Each `icon-<category>.svg/png` is the glyph alone, filling the 512 canvas with about 51 px of padding.
- To put an icon file into a frame, scale it to **0.379** (194/512) about the center. Inside `badges.py` the glyph uses a 100-unit grid drawn at 194 px.
- `set/badge-<tier>-<category>.svg/png` holds all 48 badges pre-composed. `full-set.webp` is the contact sheet.
- Display sizes: legible at 32 px. Use 64–96 pt for profile pins.

## Icons
- Body: an ivory gradient, #FFFFFF to #D9D6E3.
- Accent: a purple gradient, #C4B1FF to #7C3AED.
- Cut-out details: #1A1328.
- Rainbow chaser fan: pink #F472B6, amber #FBBF24, sky #38BDF8, violet #A78BFA. It deliberately uses **no green or red**, because those are reserved for price moves.
- Categories: Collector (card), Streak (flame), Rainbow chaser (card fan), Slab king (slab with crown), Voice of the hobby (speech bubbles), Following (people), Leaderboard (trophy), Limited (calendar with star).

## Locked (unearned) state
- The frame becomes a silhouette in #17171A, with a 3 px #2E2E33 edge. Glows and sparks are removed.
- The well is #0C0C0E.
- The icon silhouette is #3F3F46.
- The progress ring sits at r 116 with a 12 px stroke. The track is #27272A and the arc is #A78BFA. It has round caps and starts at 12 o'clock, running clockwise.
- A small lock dot sits at 6 o'clock: a #09090B disc with a #A1A1AA padlock.
- Files:
  - `locked/frame-locked-<tier>.svg/png`: a silhouette frame with no ring.
  - `locked/progress-ring-60pct.svg`: the ring on its own. Set `stroke-dasharray` to `(progress × 728.8) 728.8`.
  - `locked/locked-<tier>-<cat>.svg/png`: examples.
- `locked-state.webp` is the overview. Its numbers (3 / 5, 1 / 4, 9 / 10) are examples only.

## Legendary loop
- The loop is 48 frames at 24 fps (2.0 s), and it loops seamlessly.
- It combines two motions:
  - The aura and core glow pulse on a cosine (aura opacity .8 to 1).
  - A soft white diagonal shine band sweeps across the crest once per loop, clipped to the body.
- Files:
  - `legendary-loop.mp4`: 512 px on a #09090B background, H.264.
  - `legendary-loop.webp`: 256 px on a #09090B background, animated WebP (was a GIF).
  - `legendary-loop.png`: an animated PNG (APNG), 256 px, transparent.
  - `legendary-loop-sheet.png`: 12 frames (every 4th) in a 4 × 3 grid at 256 px, transparent.
  - `anim/f000–f047.png`: raw 512 px frames.
- In `badges.py`, `frame_legendary(t)` takes t in [0,1). `t=None` gives the static rest pose.

## Profile mock
- `profile-mock.webp` is a plain labeled layout mock, not a screenshot.
- It uses the team account (Slabhold, @slabhold) with the Slabhold logo as the avatar.
- Pins: Legendary Collector, Diamond Streak, Gold Slab king.

## Guardrails followed
- No league, team, manufacturer or TCG marks.
- No athlete likenesses.
- No selling, offers or marketplace UI.
- Green and red appear nowhere in the badges.
